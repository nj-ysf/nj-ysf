## About me

I'm **Naji Youssef**, an AI and machine-learning developer focused on turning models into practical systems.

- Building with reinforcement learning, computer vision, RAG and data analytics
- Interested in optimization, intelligent transportation and production AI
- Currently expanding my portfolio through end-to-end AI projects
- Open to collaboration on applied AI and machine-learning projects
